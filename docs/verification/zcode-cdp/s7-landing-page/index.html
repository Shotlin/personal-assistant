Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your first brew.

Can I pause or cancel my subscription?

Yes — anytime, in one click from your account. There are no fees, no phone calls, and no minimum commitment. You can also skip a month if you're still working through your stash.

How fresh is the coffee when it arrives?

Extremely fresh. Beans are roasted after you order and shipped within 48 hours, so they typically land on your doorstep three to five days off-roast — right at the start of their peak flavor window.

What if I don't like a coffee I receive?

Tell us and we'll credit your next box automatically. Your ratings also tune future picks, so the subscription gets better at matching your taste over time.
